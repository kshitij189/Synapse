# 5.1 Purpose

This chapter defines the Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs) for the Autonomous Adaptive Organization Platform (AAOP).

Whereas Chapter 4 specifies **what** the platform shall do through its functional capabilities, this chapter specifies **how well** those capabilities shall perform and the quality characteristics that shall govern the platform throughout its operational lifecycle.

The requirements defined in this chapter establish system-wide expectations for performance, scalability, availability, reliability, security, privacy, maintainability, extensibility, interoperability, usability, observability, recoverability, compliance, and other quality attributes necessary for enterprise deployment. These requirements apply across all platform components unless explicitly stated otherwise.

The objectives of this chapter are to:

* Define measurable quality expectations for the platform.
* Establish architectural and operational characteristics required for production deployment.
* Ensure consistent behavior across all functional subsystems.
* Support enterprise-grade reliability, security, and operational resilience.
* Provide a foundation for system verification, validation, testing, deployment, and ongoing operations.

The non-functional requirements contained in this chapter are technology-independent unless otherwise specified. Implementation choices, deployment architectures, operational targets, and infrastructure configurations may vary provided that the resulting system satisfies the quality objectives defined herein.

The quality attributes defined in this chapter shall be considered collectively. Compliance with a single quality attribute shall not compromise the platform's ability to satisfy the remaining quality requirements unless explicitly authorized through documented architectural decisions.

The requirements in this chapter shall be used by architects, developers, testers, DevOps engineers, security engineers, system administrators, quality assurance teams, and platform operators as the primary reference for evaluating the operational characteristics and production readiness of AAOP throughout its lifecycle.
