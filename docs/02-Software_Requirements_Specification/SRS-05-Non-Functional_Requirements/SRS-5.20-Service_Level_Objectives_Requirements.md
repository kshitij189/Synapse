# 5.20 Service Level Objectives Requirements

## 5.20.1 Purpose

Service Level Objectives (SLO) requirements define the ability of the Autonomous Adaptive Organization Platform (AAOP) to establish, monitor, evaluate, report, and continuously improve measurable operational objectives governing platform service quality.

The platform shall support definition and evaluation of service level objectives for platform services, organizational operations, integrations, and administrative capabilities while preserving organizational integrity, governance compliance, security, tenant isolation, and operational transparency.

These requirements apply to all operational platform services unless explicitly stated otherwise.

---

## 5.20.2 Service Level Objective Principles

The service level objective characteristics of AAOP shall be governed by the following principles:

* Service quality shall be measurable through objective operational indicators.
* Service level objectives shall support continuous operational evaluation.
* SLO evaluation shall rely upon observable operational information.
* Service objectives shall remain independent of specific deployment technologies.
* SLO management shall preserve organizational governance and accountability.
* Service level information shall support continuous operational improvement.
* SLO management shall remain adaptable to changing organizational requirements.

---

## 5.20.3 Business Rules

The following business rules apply to service level objectives.

* Service level objectives shall be measurable.
* SLO evaluation shall use authorized operational information.
* Service level reporting shall remain attributable and auditable.
* SLO management shall preserve tenant isolation.
* Service level evaluation shall support organizational governance activities.
* Operational objectives shall remain configurable according to deployment requirements.

## 5.20.6 Relationship to Other Quality Attributes

Service Level Objective requirements provide the framework for measuring and evaluating the operational quality of AAOP throughout its lifecycle.

In particular:

* **Performance** supplies operational metrics that contribute to evaluation of responsiveness, throughput, and efficiency objectives.
* **Availability** defines the quality expectations governing continuous service operation, while service level objectives provide measurable operational targets for those expectations.
* **Reliability** contributes operational indicators related to correctness, consistency, and dependable service behavior.
* **Observability** provides the telemetry, metrics, logs, traces, and diagnostic information required to evaluate service level objectives.
* **Capacity Planning** uses historical service level information to anticipate future resource requirements and maintain operational quality during growth.
* **Compliance** requires service level reporting and operational evidence to remain auditable and consistent with governance obligations.
* **Configuration Management** enables controlled administration of deployment-specific service level objectives and operational thresholds.

Service level objective mechanisms shall support continuous measurement and improvement of enterprise service quality while preserving organizational integrity, tenant isolation, governance compliance, and operational transparency.

## 5.20.7 Section Summary

This section defines the service level objective requirements governing AAOP.

These requirements establish expectations for measurable service objectives, operational measurement, continuous evaluation, historical service level information, reporting, threshold management, organizational and integration service objectives, administrative visibility, governance compliance, observability integration, secure handling of service level information, tenant-aware management, continuous service improvement, and configurable operational objectives. Collectively, they ensure that AAOP can define, monitor, evaluate, and continuously improve enterprise service quality using objective operational evidence while supporting diverse deployment environments and organizational requirements.
